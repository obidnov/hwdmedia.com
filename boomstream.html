Platform features

Your video workflow, covered

Manage your library, go live and sell access from a single platform.

Video hosting & player

Organize your library into projects and folders. Replace videos while keeping the same embed code.

  • Upload through your browser, FTP, TUS or API
  • Adaptive quality and delivery through a CDN
  • Branding, subtitles, chapters and playlists
Learn more

Live streams & webinars

Host public streams, private webinars and paid events on your own website.

  • Live broadcasts and recordings
  • Scheduled automated webinars and IP cameras
  • Chat and restreaming to other platforms
Learn more

Protection & access

Decide who can watch your content and where. Combine protection and access controls to suit your needs.

  • DRM encryption and watermarks
  • Domain and IP restrictions
  • Passwords, expiring links and session controls
Learn more

Content monetization

Sell video lessons, webinars and live events with payment inside the player.

  • Automatic access after payment
  • Access periods and device limits
  • Pay-per-view and sales analytics
Learn more

Boomstream Meet

Run classes and meetings in the browser. Meeting recordings are automatically added to your video library.

  • Full HD recording and screen sharing
  • Whiteboard and collaborative documents
  • Chat, polls and LMS integration
Learn more

Analytics & management

Track viewing activity and manage your library through the dashboard or programmatically.

  • Viewing and engagement statistics
  • API control of videos, folders and access
  • Webhooks for status updates
